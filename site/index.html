About

Every detail, checked on site

Ridham Patel is an interior designer based in Gandhinagar. He works hands-on at P Square Interior Studio, alongside Parth Patel, who brings nine years of interior design experience.

That means real homes and workspaces, from design and 3D views through to execution on site, and an eye for the details that decide how a space feels to live in. Being local to Gandhinagar, he’s on site whenever the job needs him.

Services

Spaces I design

Living rooms

Layouts, TV and feature walls, false ceilings and lighting that make the main room work for the whole family.

Bedrooms

Wardrobes, bed-back panels, side units and soft lighting, planned around storage and calm.

Modular kitchens

Practical layouts, durable finishes and hardware, with storage planned for how you actually cook.

Office & commercial

Workspaces, cabins and shops planned for daily use, clients and a clear brand feel.

3D design

See the room before work starts: 3D views to settle materials, colours and layout in advance.

Turnkey execution

One point of contact from civil changes to the final finish, coordinated on site until handover.

How I work

Four steps, no surprises

  1. 01

    Consultation

    A free first conversation and site visit: your needs, your budget, your timeline.

  2. 02

    Design & 3D views

    Space planning and layout, then realistic 3D views of each room, refined with you until it feels right.

  3. 03

    Execution on site

    Carpentry, electrical, finishes and fixtures, supervised on site.

  4. 04

    Handover

    Final checks, cleaning and a walkthrough of the finished space.
